Glass vs Plastic — comparison of image-classification algorithms and models

37 models and sensor configurations plus one cross-cutting component, scored on ten criteria, with the literature evidence behind each one. Static, self-contained page: every number is recomputed in your browser from an embedded copy of data/model_registry.csv, data/model_scores.csv and configs/scoring.yaml. Change the weights and the ranking moves.

How to read this page. The literature evidence quoted for each model comes from the papers listed in docs/12_references.md. Numbers measured by this repository appear only in the last section and come from a synthetic proxy renderer — they test method behaviour (robustness, failure modes), not real glass/plastic accuracy.

3. Measured behaviour on the synthetic proxy task

Proxy-data caveat. Everything below comes from the built-in renderer (gvp.synth), 600 images at 128×128 with a 70/15/15 split and seven shifted test sets. It measures how method families fail, which is transferable, rather than how well they work on glass and plastic, which is not.

4. What this dataset cannot tell you

  • Absolute accuracies: the renderer is not a camera. Use results/*.csv for relative comparisons only.
  • Cue importance: the cue ablation is underpowered (95% CIs of ±0.10 on n=120), so single-cue deltas are inside the noise floor. A factorial design with ≥1,000 test items per cell is required.
  • Real transparent-item difficulty: the renderer's clear-plastic and clear-glass renderings are separable by construction in ways that real PET and real soda-lime glass are not.
